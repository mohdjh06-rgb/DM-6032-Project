UI/UX Summer Bootcamp Pt.2

UI (User Interface) and UX (User Experience) are distinct but related fields in product design. UI focuses on the visual and interactive elements users engage with, such as buttons, colors, and layouts, while UX encompasses the user's entire experience, including how a product feels and functions from start to finish.
