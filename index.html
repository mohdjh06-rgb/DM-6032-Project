Welcome to 6032

DM6032 is a UI/UX course designed to help students design and develop websites. UI/UX design focuses on creating digital experiences that are both visually appealing and user-friendly. UI (User Interface) is about designing the look and feel of a product—its layout, colors, typography, and interactive elements—while UX (User Experience) is about ensuring the product is intuitive, efficient, and enjoyable to use. Together, UI and UX work hand in hand to deliver products that not only look good but also solve problems and meet user needs seamlessly.
